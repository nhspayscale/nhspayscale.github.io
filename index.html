How every figure is verified

NHS Pay Scale turns official Agenda for Change data into a figure you can check. Nothing is published from a press release or a news story.

Official circulars only

Every pay figure is taken from the primary source — the NHS pay circulars and official publications for each nation — across 43 primary sources.

Public change log

When a figure changes, the change is logged publicly with the reason and the source, so the history of every number stays visible.

Published corrections log

Mistakes happen; ours are corrected in the open and recorded in a published corrections log on the main site.
